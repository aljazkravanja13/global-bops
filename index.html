Global Bops

one song. one artist. every country.

About the Project

Global Bops is a hand-curated musical tour of the planet: for every country in the world, one artist and one song that captures something essential about the place, chosen one country at a time, by ear, not by algorithm.

How it works

Head to Explore and click any country on the map or the globe. Curated countries open a card with the artist, the song and a link to find it on Spotify. The rest say not curated yet — they light up as the collection grows, so it pays to come back.

    AI Disclaimer

    This site is built with AI assistance: the code for the map and globe experiences, and early drafts of country notes and song write-ups, were produced with the help of AI tools.

    Every pick is then curated — kept, changed or rejected — by a human. Still, choose with your own ears and verify artists’ origin and credits before sharing anything as fact. If a pick looks wrong for a country, it probably is; tell us and it will be re-listened to.
